Valence reads the other side of a breakup. You answer a few questions and log what your ex does; the App places your ex in a phase, writes a reading for each thing you log, and gives you a coach, the Strategist, to talk it through. Readings and coach replies are written by an AI model (see section 6 and the Privacy Policy).
