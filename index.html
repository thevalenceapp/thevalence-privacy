Privacy Policy — Last updated: July 25, 2026

Privacy First

The Valence is designed with privacy at its core. Most recovery data is stored locally on your device. When you use AI-powered features, selected data is sent for processing as described below. We also keep a minimal account record and, as of this update, collect advertising-measurement data so we can understand which of our own ad campaigns bring users to the app. This policy describes what data we collect, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

Data Storage

Most personal data — including journal entries, voice memos, mood logs, reason wall items, quiz answers, coach conversations, recovery progress, and app settings — is stored locally on your device using on-device persistence.

We maintain a minimal server-side account record (Google Firebase, using an anonymous identifier) containing: your first name if you entered one, your app language, your subscription/trial status, and basic activity timestamps used to schedule reminders. Your recovery content itself — journals, chats, voice memos, quiz answers — is not stored in this record.

AI-Powered Features and Data Processing

The Valence uses Google's Gemini API to provide the following AI-powered features: AI Mentor, Journal AI Insight, Voice Analysis, Daily Protocol, and Command Center (Weekly Report).

When you use any of these features, the following categories of data may be sent to Google Gemini for processing:

  • Content you create — such as messages, journal text, voice recordings, screenshots you choose to share, and recent conversation context when needed
  • Recovery context — such as your no-contact day count, streak, mood, stability score, urge/risk level, and check-in signals
  • Activity statistics — such as SOS usage, no-contact breaches, and journal, article, or voice activity counts
  • Personalization details — such as your first name if entered, selected coach, and active focus

AI requests are routed through Cloudflare Workers and processed by Google Gemini. Valence does not store AI inputs in its own app database.

How each AI feature uses your data

AI Mentor

When you send messages to the AI Mentor, your message text and recent conversation context are sent to Google's Gemini API. The AI generates a response based on your message and your current recovery context (such as your no-contact day count, stability score, and mood). Your name may be included in the request to personalize coaching.

Journal AI Insight

When you request an AI analysis of a journal entry, the text content of that specific entry, along with previous entries for comparison, is sent to Google's Gemini API for emotional pattern analysis. The AI returns a structured analysis (emotional pattern, risk assessment, insight, and suggested action) which is then stored locally on your device alongside the entry.

Voice Analysis

When you record a voice memo and the AI analysis runs, the audio recording is sent to Google's Gemini API for transcription and emotional analysis. The resulting transcript, mood assessment, and risk score are stored locally on your device. A visible recording indicator is displayed at all times during voice recording.

Daily Protocol

When you use the Daily Protocol's AI features, recovery context (such as your mood, stability score, streak, check-in data, and recent activity statistics) is sent to Google's Gemini API for personalized coach guidance. Results are cached locally on your device.

Command Center (Weekly Report)

When you generate an AI report in the Command Center, aggregated recovery data (such as mood patterns, check-in counts, urge levels, SOS usage, no-contact breach count, journal/voice/article counts, and active focus) is sent to Google's Gemini API. Results are cached locally on your device.

All AI processing is performed by Google's Gemini API. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's API Terms of Service and their Privacy Policy.

User Consent

A one-time AI Data Sharing consent dialog is shown before the first use of any AI-powered feature. The dialog discloses which AI features send data, what categories of data may be sent, and that data is routed through Cloudflare Workers and processed by Google Gemini.

You must tap "Allow AI Processing" to use AI features. If you tap "Not Now," no data is sent and AI features remain disabled. Non-AI features (journaling, no-contact tracking, mood logging, articles) continue to work fully.

You can manage your AI data sharing preference at any time from the Settings screen within the app.

Onboarding Quiz Data

During onboarding, you complete a personalization quiz about your breakup situation (who initiated, reason, your reaction, contact status, and recovery goals). This data is stored locally on your device and is used to:

  • Recommend the most suitable AI coaching persona for your situation
  • Personalize your first AI Mentor conversation based on your specific circumstances
  • Include relevant context in AI coaching prompts to provide more tailored guidance

Quiz answers are never sent to any server independently, and they are never shared with advertising partners. They are included as part of the AI coaching context only when you actively use AI features, under the same consent and data processing terms described above.

Voice Recordings

The Valence includes a voice recording feature for personal voice memos. In accordance with applicable requirements:

  • A clearly visible recording indicator (animated button and status label) is displayed whenever a recording is in progress.
  • Voice recordings are stored locally on your device.
  • If you choose to use AI analysis, the audio data is sent to Google's Gemini API for transcription and analysis, then the results are stored locally.
  • You can delete any voice recording at any time from within the app.

Subscriptions

The Valence offers optional premium subscriptions managed through Apple's App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat. When you subscribe:

  • Payment is processed entirely by Apple or Google. We do not collect or store your payment information.
  • RevenueCat receives an anonymous user identifier to manage your subscription status, and forwards subscription events (such as trial start, purchase, and renewal) to our advertising-measurement partner as described in the "Advertising Measurement" section below.
  • Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your device's App Store or Google Play settings.

Free Trial

Subscription plans may include a free introductory trial (currently 3 days) provided through Apple's or Google's subscription systems; trial eligibility is tracked per Apple ID / Google account. No payment information is collected by us. Free users receive a limited number of AI credits per week.

Advertising Measurement (AppsFlyer & TikTok)

We advertise The Valence on platforms such as TikTok. To understand which of our own campaigns bring users to the app, we use AppsFlyer, a mobile measurement partner. For this purpose, the following data may be collected and shared with AppsFlyer and, in aggregated or matched form, with the advertising platform (e.g., TikTok):

  • Device identifiers — on iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you grant permission via the system tracking prompt, plus the vendor identifier (IDFV); on Android, the Google Advertising ID
  • Technical information — device model, operating system version, app version, and IP address (from which an approximate, city-level location may be derived)
  • Attribution events — app installs, app opens, and subscription events (trial start, purchase, renewal) forwarded server-side via RevenueCat

This data is used solely to measure and optimize our own advertising. We never share your journal entries, coach conversations, voice recordings, screenshots, or quiz answers with advertising partners.

Your choices: On iOS, the system tracking prompt lets you decline — if you do, the IDFA is not used, and Apple's privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork framework provides only aggregate campaign results. You can change this choice anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in device Settings → Google → Ads.

AppsFlyer's handling of this data is described in the AppsFlyer Privacy Policy, and TikTok's in the TikTok for Business Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The Valence uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Gemini API — AI feature processing. Data is transmitted only when you explicitly use AI features. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare Workers — API relay for infrastructure security between the app and Google Gemini.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management using an anonymous user identifier; forwards subscription events to AppsFlyer for advertising measurement. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • AppsFlyer — mobile measurement partner for our advertising campaigns, as described above. See the AppsFlyer Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok for Business — advertising platform that receives attribution and campaign-measurement data. See the TikTok for Business Privacy Policy.

  • Apple App Store / TestFlight and Google Play — used for app distribution, billing, and beta testing. Subject to Apple's and Google's privacy policies.

What We Do NOT Do

We want to be explicit about what we do not do:

  • We do not require account creation, login, or registration — the app works with an anonymous identifier.
  • We do not collect your email address or phone number. If you email us, we use your address only to reply.
  • We do not access your contacts or calendar, and we do not collect precise (GPS) location.
  • We do not display third-party advertisements inside the app.
  • We do not sell your data, and we never share your journal entries, coach conversations, voice recordings, or quiz answers with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Since most data is stored locally on your device:

  • You can delete individual entries (journal, voice memos, reason wall items) at any time from within the app.
  • You can reset all app data using the "Reset All Data" option in Settings, which permanently erases all locally stored information.
  • Removing the app removes its locally stored app data from the device.
  • Data sent to Google's Gemini API for processing is handled according to Google's data retention policies.

Data Deletion Requests

You can delete your data in two ways:

  • In the app: Settings → Reset All Data erases everything stored on your device.
  • Server-side data: to request deletion of your server-side data (your anonymous account record, name, and usage events held by us and our measurement partner), email info@thevalence.app with the subject "Delete my data". We complete deletion requests within 30 days.

Subscription and billing records are held by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat under their own retention policies. AI content (chat messages, screenshots, voice recordings) is processed in real time and is not stored on our servers.

Data Security

All data stored on your device benefits from the security protections provided by your device's operating system, including device encryption. Communications with external APIs use HTTPS encryption. We recommend that you protect your device with a passcode or biometric authentication to further secure your data.

Children

The Valence is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app, please contact us.

Your Rights (KVKK / GDPR)

If you are located in Turkey or the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data (your recovery data is already visible to you in the app).
  • Delete your data at any time using the "Reset All Data" option in Settings, and request server-side deletion by email as described above.
  • Withdraw consent for AI processing from the Settings screen or by simply not using AI features.
  • Opt out of advertising tracking at any time: on iOS via Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, on Android via Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Request information about how your data is processed by contacting us.
